## Where billing lives

Billing is admin-only. Sign in as an admin, open your **Profile** (avatar in the header), then click **Manage** in the Billing section. That opens the Stripe billing portal — every billing change happens there.


<Info>
  Coaches and students can't see billing, even if they're paying for it personally — admins only. If you're paying for the academy from outside the school account, ask the school owner to add you as an admin first.
</Info>


## What you can do in the portal

-   Update card, expiration, and billing address.
-   Download every past invoice (Stripe also emails a PDF after each charge).
-   See your next-charge date and amount.
-   Cancel — see "Cancel" below for what that means.

## Free trial

Every account starts with a free trial. Card required to begin so we don't have to interrupt you on day one to take payment. The exact trial length lives on the [pricing page](https://www.kaynos.net/#pricing); the in-app countdown sits in Profile → Billing.

**Cancel before the trial ends → no charge.** Cancel after → you're charged through the current period and access continues until that period ends.

## Failed payment

If a charge fails (expired card, insufficient funds, fraud check), Stripe retries automatically over the next few days and emails you on each attempt. While retries are pending, your account stays active.

If every retry fails, the account moves to **past due**: read-only access stays for a short grace window so you can update the card. After that, the account suspends — data preserved, no upload/notes/share until billing is fixed.

To recover: Profile → Billing → Manage → update the card. Stripe retries on the spot and the account restores.

## Invoices & receipts

Every charge emails a Stripe receipt to the billing address. Downloadable PDF invoices are in the portal. Need a custom invoice for accounting (specific school name, tax ID, PO number)? Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) and we'll add the line items.

## Tax & VAT

Stripe calculates tax from your billing address — VAT for EU customers, sales tax for applicable US states. The invoice line items show the breakdown. With a VAT ID or tax-exempt status, add it in the billing portal before your next renewal; it applies going forward.

## Refunds

We refund pro-rata for mid-period cancellations if you're unhappy with the service. Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with your school name — refunds usually clear in 5–10 business days back to the original card.

## Cancel

Profile → Billing → Manage → Cancel subscription. Access stays available through the end of the period you've already paid for, then the account deactivates. Data is preserved for the standard [retention window](/privacy-on-this-site) after that — re-subscribe anytime to restore full access without losing recordings or notes.

## Pausing

There's no in-app pause. The functional equivalent: cancel via the portal (no next-period charge, data preserved during retention), re-subscribe when you're ready. For a longer pause where you're worried about hitting the retention window, email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) and we'll work something out.

## Adding coaches / changing seats

The plan is flat per coach. There are no tiers or seat counts to manage. Add coaches by inviting them as admin/coach in [Members](/managing-members) — Kaynos creates their account at the standard rate and Stripe prorates the change on the next invoice.

## Account deletion vs cancellation

Two different things:

-   **Cancel** stops the recurring charge. Data stays in retention; resume by re-subscribing.
-   **Delete** (Admin → Delete School) schedules account-wide deletion with a 30-day grace period. After 30 days, all data is permanently removed and re-subscribing won't recover it.


<Tip>
  Stripe never sends Kaynos your card number — payment processing happens at PCI-DSS Level 1 inside Stripe. We see the receipt, the customer email, and the brand and last-4 of the card.
</Tip>
