  <Card title="In-app chat" icon="comment" href="https://app.kaynos.net">

<Info>
  **Response time:** Usually within 24 business hours (Mon-Fri); weekend messages get the next business day. For urgent issues, in-app chat is faster - we see it in real time.
</Info>

## What to include

- **Your account name** (the academy / school)
- **What happened** - what you were trying to do and what you saw instead
- **Browser and device** (e.g., Chrome on Mac, Safari on iPhone)
- **Screenshot or screen recording**, if possible

The more context you give, the faster we can diagnose.

<Tip>
  Before emailing, try the [Troubleshooting wizard](/troubleshoot) - it covers most upload, playback, login, and performance issues in 1-2 minutes.
</Tip>
