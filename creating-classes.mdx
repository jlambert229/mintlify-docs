---
title: "Creating a class"
---
A **class** is account-wide: group sessions, workshops, or any recording for all students.

## Workflow

1.  Go to **Classes** → **New Class**.
2.  Choose your video source: **Upload** or **Notes Only**.
3.  Fill in title and date, plus an optional description and filtering tags.
4.  Click **Create Class**.

## Who sees it

All members, coaches, and admins in your account. This broad visibility is intentional — it's what separates a class from a private session.
