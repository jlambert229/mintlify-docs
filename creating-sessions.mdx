<Note>
  **TLDR:** Sessions are private (one student). Classes are shared (everyone). Create either from the dashboard after signing in.
</Note>


## Session vs Class

The most common question from new users: when do I create a session vs. a class?

Private

### Session

One-on-one content for a single student.

-   Assigned to one specific student
-   Only that student, the creator, and admins can see it
-   Private lessons, individual feedback, competition footage
-   Notes stay between coach and student

Shared

### Class

Account-wide content for everyone.

-   Visible to all members, coaches, and admins
-   Group sessions, workshops, technique breakdowns
-   Great for shared curriculum and team training
-   Everyone can leave notes

### Quick decision

-   **Is this for one person?** → Create a **session**
-   **Should everyone see it?** → Create a **class**

A **session** is one-on-one content: private lessons, individual review, or competition footage for a single student. Only that student and staff who need access can see it.

## Workflow

1.  Go to **Sessions** → **New Session**.
2.  Choose your video source: **Upload** or **Notes Only**.
3.  Fill in title and date, assign the student from your roster, and add optional tags.
4.  Click **Create Session**.

## Who sees it

The assigned student, the creator, and admins. Other students can't open someone else's private session.

## Scheduling

The **Schedule** page shows a calendar with daily, weekly, and monthly views. To schedule a session:

1.  Go to **Schedule** and click a time slot.
2.  Fill in the session details and assign a student.
3.  The system checks for conflicts with existing sessions and school hours.

Admins set operating hours under **Admin** → **School hours**. Scheduling outside those hours is still allowed.

## Review requests

Students can request a video review from any coach. A request creates a draft session the student fills out.

### How students submit a request

1.  Click **Request a review** in the student dashboard.
2.  Select a coach and describe what they want feedback on.
3.  Upload the video to the draft session.
4.  Submit the request — the coach receives a notification.

### How coaches respond

Pending requests appear on the coach dashboard under **Awaiting feedback**. Coaches **accept** or **decline** each request. Accepting converts the draft into a full session — the coach watches the video and adds notes and annotations as usual.

### If a coach declines or doesn't respond

-   Declined requests are archived in the student's request history; the student gets an email notification.
-   Pending requests don't auto-expire — they wait until a coach acts. Students can reassign to a different coach from the same screen.
-   Students can cancel a pending request before it's accepted; once accepted it becomes a session and the cancel option disappears.


<Tip>
  Review requests are the main way students initiate feedback. Coaches who enable [email notifications](/profile) get alerted as soon as a request comes in.
</Tip>


## Multiple videos per session

A session isn't limited to one video. Attach multiple clips to the same session — useful when a student sends two angles of a drill, or to compare "attempt 1" and "attempt 2" side-by-side.

-   **Add another video:** open the session and click **Add video**. Same upload flow as the first.
-   **Switch between videos:** the player has a clip rail — jump from one to the next without leaving the session.
-   **Reorder:** drag clips in the rail or use the up/down controls. The order students see updates immediately.
-   **Notes** stay tied to the clip that was active when you wrote them — switching videos doesn't move existing notes.

The same Add/Reorder/Switch flow applies to [classes](/creating-classes) with multiple recordings.
