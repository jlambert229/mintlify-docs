</Note>


## What you can do yourself

Export

Admins: **Admin → Reports & Exports** for school data (JSON) or members (CSV). Individuals: ask your admin to include you in the export, or email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) for a personal-account export.

Correct

Profile → name, email, password. Coaches edit their own notes; admins can edit any.

Delete (your account)

Profile → Delete Account. Removes you immediately; notes you wrote stay attributed for history. School admins delete the whole school from **Admin → Delete School** (30-day grace, then permanent).

Opt out

Profile → Notifications toggles email categories off. Voice dictation is opt-in per use. AI review is opt-in per video.

## What requires a request

For anything we can't expose in the UI — full data subject access, certain corrections beyond what's editable, custom retention windows, or a copy of the operational logs touching your account — email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) from the address on file.

Include:

-   The request type (access, deletion, correction, restriction, portability).
-   If you're acting on behalf of someone else (a parent/guardian for a minor; a school employee with delegated authority), describe the relationship.
-   For state-specific rights (CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA), the state you reside in.

We acknowledge within 5 business days and complete within 45 calendar days. If a request is unusually large we'll tell you up front, and we won't charge a fee for a reasonable first request.

## For parents / guardians of minors

Kaynos isn't designed for direct use by children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 in the EU (GDPR Article 8). When a school enrolls a minor, the school holds the consent obligation. As a parent:

-   Request review, correction, or deletion of your child's data by emailing [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) — we'll verify your relationship before acting.
-   You can also ask the academy directly; they have the same access via the admin panel.
-   For the day-to-day side, see the [Parent & guardian guide](/parent-guide).

## For schools (FERPA)

If you're a school or academic institution using Kaynos for graded performance review, FERPA may apply. We provide a FERPA-compliant Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request — email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) from your administrative address.

## Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Available for any business customer who needs one — schools, gyms, training facilities, EU customers. Standard DPA with Standard Contractual Clauses for EU transfers. Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) to start.

## Breach notification

If your data is affected by a security incident, you'll receive an email within 48 hours of confirmed discovery. The notice covers what was affected, what's being done about it, and what you can do.
