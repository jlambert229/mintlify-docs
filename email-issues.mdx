---
title: "Email and notification issues"
---
Most delivery problems come from spam filtering, address typos, or disabled notification settings. Preferences live under **Profile** in [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).


<Info>
  What we send: [Email notifications](/profile). Invites: [Inviting your team](/inviting-members).
</Info>


## Not receiving messages

1.  Check spam or promotions folders (Gmail often sorts automated mail here).
2.  Add the sender to your contacts or safe-senders list.
3.  Confirm the profile email is correct and notifications are enabled.

## Invite missing

-   Check spam first.
-   Confirm the address has no typo (e.g., .gmail vs .googlemail).
-   Resend from **Admin** or use Reset Password.
-   Corporate filters may require IT allowlisting; an admin can issue a manual password reset in the meantime.

## Too many emails

**Profile** → turn off the master **Email notifications** toggle to silence everything, or use the three per-event toggles (New sessions, Class recordings, Notes from instructors) to mute some and keep others. Security email (password resets, invites, email-change verifications) always sends.
