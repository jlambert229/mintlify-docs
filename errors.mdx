## Sign-in errors

Invalid email or password

The email or password didn't match. Check Caps Lock; paste the password to bypass typos. If you signed up with Google, click **Sign in with Google** rather than entering a password.

Too many attempts (HTTP 429)

The login endpoint is rate-limited per IP. Wait 5–10 minutes and try again. On a corporate network where several people retry at once, the limit can trip — try a different network or wait it out. Reset your password if you're not sure of it.

Email not verified

Your account exists but the welcome email hasn't been confirmed. Check spam and promotions for the verification link. Still missing? Ask your admin to resend the invite or email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

Account suspended

The school's subscription is past due or canceled. The admin updates billing — see [Billing & subscription](/billing).

## Upload errors

"Please select a video file"

File type isn't supported. Convert to MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio). Free tools: [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) or [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/).

File too big (5 GB limit)

Trim the clip or re-export at 1080p H.264. Most coaching content fits comfortably under 5 GB at 1080p30.

Part upload failed: 403

The signed upload URL expired (1-hour limit). Cancel and re-upload. Most common on long uploads with intermittent connectivity.

500 server error during upload

Transient issue on our side. Cancel and retry. If it persists, email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with the file size and the step it failed at.

Upload stuck at the same percent

A chunk is silently failing to retry. Cancel and re-upload. If your connection is unstable, switch to wired.

Status stuck on "uploading"

The browser closed mid-upload and the session didn't clean itself up. Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with the session name; we'll reset the status.

## Playback errors

"This video cannot be played"

Codec mismatch — usually MKV or an unusual audio codec. Try a different browser, or re-export to MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.

Black screen with audio (or audio with no video)

Codec mismatch on one of the tracks. Re-export as standard MP4 H.264/AAC and re-upload.

Long buffering or stuttering

Check bandwidth (download speed). Very high-bitrate 4K sources can stutter on standard home networks — a 1080p H.264 export plays more reliably.

403 / "not authorized to view this video"

The playback URL expired (4-hour limit) or your role changed. Refresh the page; the player generates a fresh presigned link.

"This clip is no longer available" (shared link)

The coach disabled sharing on the clip. Ask them to re-enable it — same URL works again.

## Recording / camera errors

"Camera permission denied"

iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Camera → set Kaynos to Allow. Android Chrome: tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → Camera → Allow.

"Microphone permission denied"

Same path as camera. Required for voice dictation; not required for video upload.

"Could not access camera"

Another app or tab is holding the camera (FaceTime, Zoom, another open browser window). Close them, refresh, and try again.

## Payment errors

"Your card was declined"

Stripe rejected the charge. Common reasons: expired card, insufficient funds, fraud-check from the issuing bank, or a country mismatch. Try a different card or contact your bank. Full detail in Profile → Billing → Manage.

"This card requires authentication" (3DS)

European cards trigger Strong Customer Authentication. Complete the bank prompt; Stripe retries automatically.

Subscription past due

One or more charges failed and the retry window has elapsed. Update your card in Profile → Billing → Manage. The account un-pauses on the next successful charge.

## Email errors

Invite email never arrives

See [Email and notification issues](/email-issues). Usually: spam folder, address typo, or a corporate filter.

Password reset link expired

Reset links are valid for 1 hour. Request a new one from the login page (**Forgot password?**) or ask an admin to issue a manual reset.

Email change verification expired

Verification links live 24 hours. Re-request from Profile → Email → Send verification.

## Generic errors

"Something went wrong"

Transient client-side error. Refresh once. If it persists, screenshot what you clicked and email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

Red error banner across the top

A network request failed. Check your internet, refresh, and retry. Persistent? [netlifystatus.com](https://www.netlifystatus.com/) covers the hosting status; for the app itself, email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).
