<Note>

</Note>

Your feedback shapes what we build. Here's the fastest way to get an idea in front of us.

## Send a feature request

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email us" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@kaynos.net?subject=Feature%20request">
    [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net?subject=Feature%20request) - include what you want to do, who it's for, and how important it is.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat in-app" icon="comment" href="https://app.kaynos.net">
    Bottom-right corner in [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) when logged in - fastest path for active customers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What to include

- **What you want to do** in one sentence
- **Who it's for** (you, your students, parents, admins)
- **How important** - nice-to-have, would-use-regularly, or blocking your workflow
- **Workaround you're using today**, if any

The more context you give, the easier it is for us to prioritize.

<Tip>
  Found a doc that's wrong, missing a step, or could be clearer? Use the **thumbs down** and **Suggest edits** controls at the bottom of any page - those go directly to our docs queue.
</Tip>

## What happens next

Every request is reviewed by the team. We don't reply to every one individually, but if we have questions or build what you asked for, you'll hear from us at the email you provided.
