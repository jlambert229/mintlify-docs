<Note>
  **TLDR:** MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio) is the safe default. MOV, WebM, and MKV also work. Maximum file size is 5 GB.
</Note>


Use common codecs your devices and browsers handle well. When in doubt, export to MP4 (H.264 + AAC). Free converters: [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr), [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/).

**MP4** is the most predictable for uploads and playback. iPhone MOV usually works well. AVI is not supported — convert to MP4 first.

## File size

-   Maximum **5 GB** per file.
-   Files under **2 GB** upload faster on home or gym Wi-Fi (5 GB is the hard cap).

## Smaller files, same teaching value

-   **1080p** is enough for most instruction — high bitrates slow uploads without visible benefit.
-   Prefer **H.264** video and **AAC** audio when your export tool offers a choice.
-   Trim dead air at the start or end before uploading.

  ### Tips for faster uploads

  -   Use a **wired connection** instead of Wi-Fi when possible
  -   Pause cloud sync tools (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud)
  -   Upload during off-peak hours
  -   **1080p** is enough — 4K files are much larger with little benefit for coaching review
