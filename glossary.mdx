Key terms used throughout Kaynos and this help center.

Session

A one-on-one recording assigned to a single student — private lessons, individual review, or competition footage. Only the assigned student, the creator, and admins can see it.

Class

An account-wide recording visible to everyone — group sessions, workshops, or shared recordings. All members, coaches, and admins can view classes.

Admin

The highest-permission role. Admins can do everything coaches can, plus manage the roster, send invites, change roles, and access the Admin area.

Coach

A staff role that can create sessions and classes, upload video, add notes and tags, and view student progress. Coaches can invite students but not other coaches.

Student `member`

An end user who watches assigned sessions and all published classes, adds notes, and tracks their own progress. Students cannot upload video or view other students' private sessions.

Note

A short text annotation pinned to a specific timestamp on the video timeline. Coaches and students can both create notes; coaches can edit any note.

Tag

A label attached to sessions or classes for organization and filtering. Tags are shared across the account — examples: `guard`, `comp-prep`, `fundamentals`.

Progress

A lightweight tracker that records playback position (for resume), completion status, and last-viewed time. Coaches see completion across their students; no invasive analytics.

Clip

A short excerpt from a session or class, created by a coach to highlight a specific moment or technique. Clips can be shared externally via a token link — no login required for the viewer.

Review Request

A student-initiated request for a coach to review a video. The student picks a coach, adds context, and uploads to a draft session. The coach accepts or declines from their dashboard.

Highlight

A time-range bookmark on the video timeline, marking a start and end point. Used to call attention to a segment worth reviewing.

Annotation

A drawing overlay on a frozen video frame — pen, line, circle, or arrow — attached to a specific timestamp. Appears during playback to highlight technique details.

AI review

An optional, on-demand analysis of a session video that suggests coaching notes from what the model sees. Powered by Twelve Labs. Suggestions arrive as regular notes flagged "AI suggestion" — coaches can edit, keep, or delete them.

Presigned URL

A short-lived, signed link to a video file in Backblaze B2 storage. Kaynos serves video through presigned URLs rather than a CDN — each request generates a fresh link scoped to that viewer.

## BJJ positions and movements

New to martial arts? Here's what your coach means:

Guard

A position where one person is on their back, legs controlling the other. Many types: closed, half, open, butterfly.

Mount

A dominant position with one person sitting on the other's torso — very strong for the top.

Side control

A dominant position where one person pins the other from the side, chest-to-chest.

Back control

A dominant position behind the opponent, typically with hooks (legs wrapped around the waist).

Sweep

A technique to reverse positions — going from bottom (guard) to top.

Submission

A technique that forces the opponent to "tap out" — joint locks (armbar, kimura) or chokes (rear naked choke, guillotine, triangle).

Takedown

A technique to bring a standing opponent to the ground.

Drilling

Repetitive practice of a specific technique with a cooperative partner. Think of it like running drills in basketball.

Sparring / rolling

Live practice at varying intensity. "Rolling" is the BJJ term for sparring.
