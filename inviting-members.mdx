title: "Inviting your team"
---
Admins invite coaches and students; coaches invite students. Invitees get an email with a temporary password. Manage invites in [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) under **Admin** or **Students**.


<Info>
  Role capabilities are summarized on [Understanding user roles](/roles).
</Info>


## Send an invite

1.  Go to **Admin** (full roster control) or **Students** (coaches adding a student).
2.  Click **Add Member** / **Add Student**.
3.  Enter name, email, and role (admins can also set coach vs student).
4.  Choose how to deliver the invite:
    -   **Email invite** — Kaynos generates a temporary password and emails it to the new member.
    -   **Generate link** — Kaynos creates a single-use setup link that expires after 7 days. No password is sent; the new member sets their own when they open the link. Use this to hand off the link directly (in person, in chat) instead of relying on email.
5.  Click **Send Invite** (or **Generate link**).

Email delivery is typically quick. If nothing arrives, check spam and verify the address for typos.


<Info>
  Still missing mail? See [Email and notification issues](/email-issues) or contact [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).
</Info>

## What each role can do

Role

What they can do

**Admin**

Everything a coach can do, plus roster management, invites, and settings.

**Coach**

Sessions, classes, video upload, notes, tags, and student progress.

**Student**

Their assigned private sessions, all published classes, notes, progress tracking. No uploads; cannot view other students' private sessions.

## Resend or reset

**Admin** → find them in the roster → **Reset Password**. Kaynos emails a fresh temporary password.

## Tips

-   Onboard coaches before adding a large student wave when possible.
-   Use an email address people check — it becomes their login.
-   No fixed seat limit.
