---
title: "Login and access issues"
---
Most issues come down to the wrong email, typos, or Caps Lock. Repeated failed attempts from the same IP trigger a brief rate limit (see "Too many attempts" below). Sign in at [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).

## Password problems

-   Use the same email the invite was sent to.
-   Check Caps Lock; paste the password once to catch hidden typos.
-   Ask an admin for a reset or email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

## "Too many attempts"

After several failed attempts from the same IP, sign-ins are rate limited (HTTP 429). Wait a few minutes and try again, or reset your password. Still stuck? Contact [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

## Blank page or "no access"

The account may have been removed, or the browser cached an old session. Try a private/incognito window. If that works, clear site data for [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) in your normal browser.

## Still locked out

Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with your name, account name, and email address.

### Reset link expired

Password reset links expire after **1 hour**. If yours no longer works, request a new one from the login page (**Forgot password?**) or ask your admin to resend from **Admin → Members → Reset Password**.
