<Note>
  **TLDR:** Admins manage the full roster from **Admin**. Coaches manage their own students from **Students**. Use the role dropdown to change permissions and the **Remove** button to revoke access — notes the member created stay for history.
</Note>


Admins use **Admin** for the full roster. Coaches use **Students** to manage their own student invites. Everything happens in [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).

## Add someone

See [Inviting your team](/inviting-members) for the invite flow.

## Change roles

**Admin** → select the member → role dropdown → save. Changes apply immediately. Details: [Understanding user roles](/roles).

## Remove access

**Remove Member** ends access immediately. Notes they created remain for history. You can also remove a student from their profile page (`/students/<id>`) with the **Remove** button — admin only, mirrors the roster flow.


<Info>
  Removal is immediate — the person loses login access to your account on [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) right away.
</Info>


## Activity indicator and email privacy

Student avatars in the roster show an activity dot: **green** when the student had a session in the last 30 days, **dim** when activity is older than 30 days, no dot when they have no sessions yet.

For screen-sharing or recording, toggle **Hide emails** in the Members section. Addresses are masked to first letter + domain (`j****@example.com`); search still works against the full address.

## Common questions

**One person, two accounts?** Use a different email per account. Each account is fully separate.

**Member limits?** No headcount cap.

**Student-to-student invites?** Only staff roles can invite; students cannot.

## Multiple schools

Kaynos supports multiple schools under one account. Multi-school setup is handled by a tenant admin or by contacting [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) — not self-service. Each school has its own roster, sessions, classes, and storage quota.

## Branding

Upload a custom logo for your academy under **Admin → Branding**. Members see it in the header after they log in. PNG or SVG, square, at least 200×200px.

## School hours

Set operating hours under **Admin → School hours**. Default is 6 AM to 9 PM. The schedule view uses these hours for the daily/weekly grids; sessions can still be booked outside the window.

## Usage reports

Once your school has at least five sessions or classes, admins can receive automated email reports summarizing activity.

1.  Go to **Admin → Reports & Exports**.
2.  Choose frequency: **off**, **weekly**, or **monthly**.
3.  Reports cover the previous period — sessions and classes created, active students, storage usage.

Click **Send now** to trigger an immediate report.

## Admin panel overview

The Admin panel has several sections. Here's what each does, in order:

Academy

School name and subscription status (active, trialing, past due, cancelled). Edit the academy name from here.

Members

View roster, add/remove members, change roles, reset passwords, filter by role, and toggle email privacy mode for screen sharing.

School hours

Set operating hours used by the schedule view (default 6 AM–9 PM).

Branding

Upload a custom logo for the in-app header.

Storage

Monitor video count and storage usage against your limits (default: 100 videos, 50 GB). View expiring videos. Request a storage increase.

Reports & Exports

Configure automated usage reports (off/weekly/monthly), send a manual report, and export school data as JSON or members as CSV. Visible once your school has at least 5 sessions or classes.

Danger Zone

Schedule account deletion (30-day grace period) or cancel a pending deletion. Collapsed by default; auto-expanded if a deletion is already scheduled.

Billing (manage subscription, update payment method) lives on the signed-in admin's **Profile** page, not under Admin.
