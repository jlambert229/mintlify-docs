Attach short notes to exact moments on the timeline so everyone lands on the same frame.

## Adding a note from the player

1.  Pause the video.
2.  Click **Add Note**, type your text, then **Save** (or press <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>).

The note saves at the current playhead position. Markers appear on the scrub bar; click any to jump.

## Filters

**All** shows every note; **Coach** shows coach notes only; **Mine** shows only the signed-in student's notes.

## Who can edit

Coaches can edit any note. Students can edit only notes they created.

## @Mentions

Type **@** followed by a name to mention a teammate. Mentioned users get an email with a link straight to the note.

Use mentions to loop in a coach ("@Marco take a look at this sweep attempt") or prompt a student to review ("@Sofia check the guard retention at 2:14").

## Threaded replies

Reply to any note to start a thread. Click **Reply**, type your response, and save. The original author and anyone @mentioned get an email notification.

Threads keep feedback organized — coach observation, student question, and follow-up all stay tied to the same timestamp.

## Tips

-   Short, specific lines (technique name + brief observation) scan better later than long paragraphs.
-   Students can leave questions on their own sessions for coach follow-up.
-   Use the coach filter to review your notes across recordings.

## Note types

When creating a note, choose a type:

-   **Note** — general observation (default)
-   **Highlight** — a time-range bookmark on the timeline (start and end points)
-   **Annotation** — a drawing overlay on a paused frame (see the *Annotations* section)

AI review may also add notes flagged as **AI suggestions** — edit or delete them like any other note.

## Voice dictation

Dictate notes hands-free using speech-to-text (powered by Deepgram).

1.  The first time, grant microphone access and consent to audio processing.
2.  Click the microphone icon in the note editor and speak — text appears in real time.
3.  Edit the transcription if needed, then save.

Voice dictation is opt-in per user. Audio is processed in real time and not stored.

## Tags

Tags group related sessions and classes so list views stay scannable as your library grows.

### Tag ideas

-   Format: `private`, `group`, `workshop`, `open-mat`
-   Level: `fundamentals`, `advanced`, `beginner`
-   Focus: `comp-prep`, `conditioning`, `drilling`

### Adding tags

In the session or class form, type a tag and press **Enter**. Repeat for more.

### Finding content

Filter list views by tag — for example, everything tagged `milestone` for a training block.

### Consistency tips

-   Use one spelling per concept (avoid `guard-pass`, `Guard Pass`, and `guard_pass` for the same thing).
-   Agree on a shared vocabulary so the whole team uses consistent tags.
-   Tags are shared across the account, not private.


<Info>
  Filter your lists by tag in the app after you [sign in](https://app.kaynos.net).
</Info>


### Building a technique vocabulary

Tags are free-form text — there's no built-in taxonomy. Build a shared vocabulary the team will use consistently. Common starting points for grappling: positions (`guard`, `mount`, `side-control`), actions (`sweep`, `pass`, `escape`), and specific techniques (`armbar`, `triangle`). The exact list is up to you.

To find all sessions with a tag, filter the session list by that tag.

## Annotations

Draw on a video frame to highlight technique details.

1.  Pause the video at the moment you want to annotate.
2.  Click the annotation tool in the note editor.
3.  Draw with pen, line, circle, or arrow on the frozen frame. Pick stroke colour and width; undo/redo are available.
4.  Save — the annotation attaches to that timestamp and appears during playback.

## AI review

Kaynos can analyze your video automatically to identify key moments and generate technique observations.

1.  Open a session or class detail page.
2.  Click **Start AI Review**.
3.  Processing runs in the background — watch the status indicator.
4.  When done, AI-generated notes appear on the timeline alongside your manual notes.

AI review uses TwelveLabs for video understanding. Edit AI notes like any other note.

## Reactions

React to any note with an emoji: 👍 ❤️ 🔥 👀 ✅ 👏. Click the reaction button under a note.
