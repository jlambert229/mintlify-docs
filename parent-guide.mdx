This guide is for parents and guardians whose children train at an academy using Kaynos. You don't need any background in martial arts or the app — this page covers your child's experience and answers common questions. For the student's-eye view of the app itself, the [student guide](/student-guide) walks through the actual screens.

## How your child's account works

Kaynos has three roles: **admin**, **coach**, and **student** — there is no separate parent or guardian account. Your child's coach or academy admin creates and manages their student account.

-   Your child logs in at [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) with credentials provided by the academy.
-   The coach or admin controls what content is available to students.
-   To see what your child sees, ask their coach — they can walk you through it or share details directly.

## What your child can see and do

-   **Sessions:** Private one-on-one recordings between your child and their coach. Only the coach and your child can view these.
-   **Classes:** Group class recordings shared with students in that class. Think of these as class replays your child can re-watch to practice at home.
-   **Clips:** Short excerpts from sessions or classes that the coach has created to highlight key moments or techniques.
-   **Notes:** Timestamped coaching feedback attached to specific moments in a video. Your child can click a note to jump to that exact point in the recording.
-   **Review requests:** Your child can flag a moment in a video and ask their coach for feedback on it.

## Understanding the sport

If your child trains Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) or another martial art, some terminology in coaching notes may be unfamiliar. Common terms:

-   **Guard:** A position where a person is on their back, using their legs to control or defend against the person on top.
-   **Mount:** A dominant position where one person sits on top of the other's torso.
-   **Sweep:** A technique used from the bottom to reverse positions and end up on top.
-   **Submission:** A hold or lock (like an armbar or choke) that forces the opponent to "tap out" and concede.
-   **Rolling / sparring:** Live practice with a partner, applying techniques at varying intensity.
-   **Drilling:** Practicing a single technique repeatedly with a cooperative partner to build muscle memory.

For a more complete list, see our [glossary of BJJ terms](/glossary) — written for people who don't train.

## Privacy and safety

Video recordings of children require careful handling. How Kaynos protects your child's data:

-   **Videos are private:** Only people within your academy's Kaynos account can see recordings. Nothing is posted publicly or shared outside the school.
-   **Secure storage:** Video is stored securely in the US (Backblaze B2) and delivered over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
-   **Links expire:** Playback links are temporary and cannot be shared with or bookmarked by outsiders.
-   **No public profiles:** Students do not have public-facing profiles, pages, or any content visible outside their academy.

For the full privacy policy, see [app.kaynos.net/privacy](https://app.kaynos.net/privacy). For details on COPPA and FERPA compliance, see [Privacy on this site](/privacy-on-this-site).


<Tip>
  **Data requests:** Parents can request access to or deletion of their child's data at any time. Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) or ask your child's coach to connect you with academy administration.
</Tip>


## Questions or concerns?

With no parent login, the best way to stay informed is to talk to your child's coach or academy admin. They can:

-   Show you what your child has been working on
-   Explain any coaching notes or feedback
-   Answer questions about class content or training
-   Help with any account or access issues

You can also email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with questions about Kaynos itself — privacy, data handling, or how the platform works.
