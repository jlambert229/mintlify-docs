---
title: "Performance and loading"
---
Slow loads usually come from local network conditions, browser cache, or extensions — not a full outage.

## General slowness

1.  Aim for about 5 Mbps down for smooth 1080p playback. Upload speeds: see [Uploading videos](/uploading).
2.  Clear cache and site data for [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).
3.  Try Chrome or Firefox if Safari misbehaves.
4.  Disable strict ad or script blockers to test.
5.  Close excess tabs to free memory.

## Unstyled or broken layout

CSS likely failed to load. Hard refresh: **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac).

## Red error messages

-   Refresh once — a single failed request can trigger an error.
-   If it repeats, screenshot it, note what you clicked, and email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

## Mobile tips

Kaynos runs in the mobile browser — no app store install. Open [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) in your phone's browser.


<Info>
  Upload troubleshooting: [Uploading videos](/uploading).
</Info>


### Supported mobile browsers

-   iPhone / iPad: Safari or Chrome
-   Android: Chrome or Firefox

### Upload from a phone

Tap upload and choose from Photos or Files. Keep the screen on and stay on the tab — backgrounding the browser pauses uploads.

### Watching video on mobile

-   Tap the video to play/pause; controls hide after a few seconds.
-   Landscape often fits the frame better than portrait.
-   Playback speed, notes, and timeline markers match desktop.

### Add to home screen

-   **iOS:** Share button → Add to Home Screen
-   **Android:** browser menu → Add to Home Screen

### Mobile limits

-   Very large files on older phones can be slow or unstable — a laptop is more reliable.
-   Landscape helps when the notes panel is open on a small display.
