<Note>
  **TLDR:** Open a session or class and press play. Use <kbd>Space</kbd> to pause, <kbd>J</kbd>/<kbd>L</kbd> to skip 10s, <kbd>F</kbd> for fullscreen. Click **Add Note** to leave timestamped feedback.
</Note>


Playback runs in the browser — no app install needed. Open recordings at [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).


<Tip>
  Problems playing video? See the troubleshooting section below.
</Tip>


## Playback speed

Use **0.5×**–**2×** for slow-motion analysis or faster skimming.

## Timeline markers

Markers on the timeline are notes. Hover to preview, click to jump to that moment.

## Add a note while watching

1.  Pause the video.
2.  Click **Add Note**, enter your text, then **Save**.

The note saves at the current playhead position.


<Accordion title="Keyboard shortcuts">
  **Player controls** (when the video has focus):

  Play / Pause<kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd>

  Skip back / forward 10s<kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>L</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>

  Fullscreen<kbd>F</kbd>

  Mute / unmute<kbd>M</kbd>

  Volume up / down<kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd>

  **Inside the note editor** (after clicking Add Note):

  Save note<kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>

  Cancel<kbd>Esc</kbd>
</Accordion>


## Mobile playback

Kaynos runs in the mobile browser — no app install needed.

-   **Skip:** Double-tap the left half to skip back 10 seconds, the right half to skip forward.
-   **Landscape:** Rotate your phone for a wider view, especially with the notes panel open.
-   **Resume:** Playback position syncs across devices — start on your phone, continue on your laptop.
-   **Add to Home Screen:** iOS: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: browser menu → Add to Home Screen.

## Progress tracking

Playback position saves automatically. Coaches see completion percentage and last-viewed time without heavy analytics.

## Clips

Create a clip from any session or class to highlight a specific moment.

1.  While watching, set the start and end time for the clip.
2.  Click **Create Clip**. The clip saves to your clips list.
3.  Toggle **Share** to generate a shareable link anyone can view — no login required.

Manage clips from the session or class detail page. **Disable sharing** at any time — every existing link stops working immediately (returns 404), so a leaked link is recoverable without rotating tokens. Re-enabling later restores access using the same link.

### Recap clips

Create 2–3 minute recap clips from full class recordings so students see the highlights without watching the whole 60 minutes.

1.  Open the class recording and play to the key moment.
2.  Set the clip start time, then play to the end of the segment and set the end time.
3.  Click **Create Clip** and give it a descriptive title (e.g., "Guard retention drill — key details").
4.  Toggle **Share** to make it visible to all students in the class.

Students see recap clips on their dashboard alongside full recordings. Short clips get much higher watch rates.

### Sharing clips externally

Share a clip with someone who doesn't have a Kaynos account — a training partner at another gym, a parent, or a competition coach.

1.  Create a clip and toggle **Share** on.
2.  Copy the shareable link. Anyone with the link can view the clip — no login required.
3.  Toggle sharing off to revoke access — old links return 404 instantly; no token rotation or new URL needed.

Shared clips show the video segment only — no notes, no account details, no other sessions.


<Accordion title="Playback troubleshooting">
  If the player is blank or showing errors, work through these checks in order.


  <Info>
    Codecs: [Supported formats and limits](/formats).
  </Info>


  ### Player blank or broken controls

  **Black screen / no controls:** refresh, then hard refresh or clear cache, then try [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/). Confirm the upload finished (not stuck on "uploading" or showing "failed").

  **"Cannot be played":** often a codec or browser issue. MKV files use codecs that vary by browser — try [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/) or [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/), or re-export to MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.

  ### Buffering or long spinners

  -   Check your bandwidth (upload and download can differ dramatically by network).
  -   Close other streaming tabs or heavy downloads.
  -   High-bitrate 4K sources can stutter on typical home connections — a 1080p H.264 export plays more reliably and is enough for coaching review.

  ### Sound but no picture (or the reverse)

  Usually a codec mismatch. Try another browser; if it persists, re-export with standard H.264/AAC.

  ### Resume position wrong

  Progress saves shortly after pausing. Closing the tab immediately can skip the save.
</Accordion>
