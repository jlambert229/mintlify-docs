<Note>
  **TLDR:** Open your profile from the sidebar (desktop) or the avatar in the top-right (mobile). Update your name, email, and password from there. Notification preferences and admin-only billing live on the same page.
</Note>


## Open your profile

Desktop: click your name in the sidebar. Mobile: tap the avatar in the top-right of the header, then choose **Profile**.

## Name and details

Update your display name, then click **Save changes**.

## Email address

Your email is your sign-in identifier. To change it:

1.  In the **Email address** section, click **Change**.
2.  Enter the new address and click **Send verification**. Kaynos emails a verification link to it.
3.  Open the link to confirm. Your sign-in email updates only after the link is opened.

While a change is pending, the new address shows as **Pending** with a **Cancel pending change** button. Verification links expire after **24 hours** — request a new one if it lapses.

## Change password

1.  Click **Change Password**.
2.  Enter your current password.
3.  Enter your new password (minimum **8 characters**) and confirm it.
4.  Click **Update Password**.

Locked out? Ask an admin for a reset, or email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net). Login help: [Login and access issues](/login-issues).

## Billing (admins only)

Admins see a **Billing** section with the current subscription status and a **Manage** button that opens the Stripe billing portal in a new tab. Update payment methods, download invoices, or change plans there. Full reference on [Billing & subscription](/billing).

## Notifications

Email notifications live in the profile **Notifications** section. A master **Email notifications** toggle sits at the top, with three per-event toggles underneath:

-   **New sessions** — when a coach creates a session for you.
-   **Class recordings** — when a class recording is posted to your school.
-   **Notes from instructors** — when a coach adds a note on your session.

Turning the master switch off silences all three. With the master on, flip each sub-toggle individually — for example, keep **Notes from instructors** on but turn off class-recording emails if you only want personal coaching alerts.

**Always sends regardless of toggles:** welcome emails, password resets, invites, email-change verification links, deletion notices, and support form confirmations. Admins also receive automated usage reports on the schedule set under **Admin → Reports & Exports**.


<Tip>
  Delivery problems? See [Email and notification issues](/email-issues).
</Tip>


## In-app notifications

The bell icon in the header shows unread notifications: mentions, note replies, session shares, review requests, and other activity. Click to view, or mark all as read. On mobile, dismiss the panel with the close button.

### Not receiving mail

1.  Check spam or promotions folders.
2.  Confirm the profile email address is correct.
3.  Confirm notifications are enabled in your profile settings.
4.  If it still fails, contact [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with the expected address.
