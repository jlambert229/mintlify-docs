<Note>
  **TLDR:** For each session and class we record last playback position, completion status, and last-viewed time. Members resume where they left off. Coaches and owners filter by student and time range — Week, Month, or Quarter.
</Note>


Progress is simple on purpose: enough to see whether someone finished a recording and when they last opened it.

## What we store

For each session and class, we track last playback position (for resume), whether the full video was completed, and the last-viewed time. Coaches and admins can filter progress reports by student and time range: **Week** (7 days), **Month** (30 days), or **Quarter** (90 days).

## For members

Members resume where they left off. Overviews show items that still need a full watch versus completed items.

## For coaches and owners

Coach and admin views show completion and recency. No fine-grained analytics or viewer heatmaps.

## What we intentionally avoid

No invasive watch analytics or third-party ad-style tracking. The product stores only what it needs for resume and high-level completion.

## Dashboard metrics

The progress dashboard shows three headline numbers for the selected period:

-   **Watch rate** — percentage of assigned sessions the student has viewed.
-   **Session count** — total sessions (private and class) in the period.
-   **Note count** — total coaching notes written on the student's sessions.

## Time-period filtering

Filter buttons narrow the view to the **last 7, 30, or 90 days**. Default is 30 days.

## Per-session details

Each row in the recent-sessions list shows session title, date, a check icon if the full video was watched, and the note count. Resume position and last-viewed timestamp are tracked behind the scenes so playback picks up where it left off.
