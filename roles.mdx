---
<Note>
  **TLDR:** Three roles — **admin** manages members and settings, **coach** uploads video and writes notes, **student** watches and replies. Pick the one that matches how each person uses Kaynos.
</Note>


Each person has one of three roles. Assign them so permissions match how your team runs.

## Admin

Typically the school owner or head coach. Full access: everything a coach can do, plus member management, invites, role changes, account settings, and the **Admin** panel for account-wide visibility.

## Coach

Coaches create sessions and classes, upload video, add notes and annotations, manage their own content, and view student progress. They can't manage members or access admin settings.

## Student

Students watch sessions and classes shared with them, take notes, reply, request video review from coaches, and track their own progress. They can't create sessions or classes, upload video, or view other students' private sessions.

## Change a role

Admins only: **Admin** → select the member → role dropdown → save.


<Info>
  Downgrading a coach to student removes the ability to create sessions or upload video. Existing content stays available. For invite rules, see [Inviting your team](/inviting-members).
</Info>
