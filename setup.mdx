## New school signup

Three steps to create a school account:

1.  **Sign up** — enter your school name, your name, and email at [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).
2.  **Payment** — see [current pricing on kaynos.net](https://www.kaynos.net/#pricing) for plan price, what's included, and free-trial terms. A card is required to start the trial; cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.
3.  **Confirmation** — you land on the coach dashboard with the instructor checklist.

The **instructor checklist** walks you through three first actions — add a student, record a session, upload a class — at your own pace.

## Invited members

If a coach or admin invited you, login details go to the email address they used. Change the temporary password as soon as you're in.

### First login

1.  Use the welcome email link or go to [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net).
2.  Enter that email and the temporary password from the invite.
3.  You land on the **Coach Dashboard** — home base.

### Google login

If your account was created with Google, click **Sign in with Google** on the login page. No separate password — your Google account handles authentication.

## Set a new password

Secure your own account before inviting your full roster.

1.  Click your name in the sidebar (hamburger on mobile) → **Profile**.
2.  **Change Password**: enter current, new, then confirm.
3.  Hit **Update Password**. Done.

## Admin area

Admins: use **Admin** in the sidebar for roster, invites, role changes, and an account-wide activity view. Explore these screens before sending a large batch of invites.


<Accordion title="Quick tips">
  Visual step-by-step guides for the most common tasks in Kaynos.

  ### Upload a video


  <Steps>
    <Step title="Create a session or class">
      Go to **Sessions** or **Classes** and click the **New** button.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose &quot;Upload&quot; as source">
      Select **Upload** when prompted.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Drag or select your file">
      Drop the video on the upload area, or click to browse. MP4 recommended.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Wait for the green check">
      The progress bar shows speed and ETA. Green check = done. Fill in title, date, and save.
    </Step>
  </Steps>


  ### Add a timestamped note


  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open the session or class">
      Navigate to the recording you want to annotate.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Play to the right moment">
      Use <kbd>J</kbd>/<kbd>L</kbd> to skip, or click the timeline to jump.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Add Note">
      The video pauses. Type your observation.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
      The note appears as a timeline marker. Hover to preview, click to jump back.
    </Step>
  </Steps>


  ### Invite a student


  <Steps>
    <Step title="Go to Admin or Students">
      Admins use **Admin** for full control. Coaches use **Students**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Add Member / Add Student">
      Enter their name, email, and role.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Send the invite">
      They get an email with a temporary password. Delivery is typically quick.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  </Tip>
