<Note>
  **TLDR:** Sign in at [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net). Watch sessions assigned to you, watch class recordings, leave notes, ask for review. Everything is private to your academy.
</Note>


This page is written for students — the people watching, learning, and getting reviewed. If you're a coach or admin, the [coach handbook](/coach-handbook) or the [admin pages](/managing-members) are a better fit.

## First sign-in

1.  Open the welcome email from your coach. The temporary password is there.
2.  Go to [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) and sign in.
3.  Change the password under [Profile](/profile). Make it something only you know.

## What's on your dashboard

-   **Sessions** — private one-on-one recordings between you and a coach.
-   **Classes** — recordings shared with everyone in the academy.
-   **Clips** — short highlights the coach has pulled out.
-   **Notifications** (bell icon, top of header) — opens when a coach leaves a note or replies to a thread.

## Watching a session or class

Click into one to open the player. Notes show up as markers on the timeline — hover (or tap on phone) to preview, click to jump. Use <kbd>Space</kbd> to play/pause, <kbd>J</kbd>/<kbd>L</kbd> to skip 10s. Full controls on [Watching and reviewing](/playback).

## Asking for review

If you recorded yourself and want a coach to look at it:

1.  Click **Request a review** on your dashboard.
2.  Pick a coach. Add a sentence on what you want feedback on ("technique on the closed-guard sweep — it feels off and I can't tell why").
3.  Upload the video to the draft session that's created.
4.  Submit. The coach gets an email.

You'll get an email back when the coach responds. If they decline or don't respond, re-assign to a different coach from the same screen.

## Leaving notes

While watching anything shared with you — your own session or a class — pause and click **Add Note**. Notes pin to the exact timestamp. Type a question for your coach, a reminder to yourself, whatever.

Mention a teammate or coach with **@name** — they get an email with a link to that specific moment.

## Tuning notifications

Profile → Notifications. Three categories:

-   **New sessions** — when a coach creates a session for you.
-   **Class recordings** — when a class recording is posted.
-   **Notes from instructors** — when a coach adds a note on one of your sessions.

Each one toggles independently. Password resets and invites always send regardless.

## What you can't do (by design)

-   Upload class recordings (only coaches/admins).
-   See other students' private sessions.
-   Invite people to the academy.
-   Change the academy logo or settings.

You can always upload to your own session via a review request, and you can take notes on anything shared with you.

## On your phone

Open [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) in your phone's browser — no separate app to install. Or add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS; browser menu → Add to Home Screen on Android) for an app-like icon. See [Using Kaynos on mobile](/using-on-mobile).

## Privacy

Everything you do — recordings, notes, replies — is visible only inside your academy. Nothing is public. Full description on [Privacy on this site](/privacy-on-this-site), or the [parent guide](/parent-guide) for a less technical version.
