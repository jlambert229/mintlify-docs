<Accordion title="Browser compatibility check">
  Kaynos works in any modern browser. Here's what yours looks like:

  ## Recommended browsers

  Chrome (recommended) Full support

  Firefox Full support

  Safari Good (use MP4 with H.264 for best compatibility)

  Edge Full support

  ## If something doesn't work

  1.  Try Chrome — it has the broadest codec and feature support
  2.  Hard refresh: <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd> (Windows/Linux) or <kbd>Cmd+Shift+R</kbd> (Mac)
  3.  Clear site data for app.kaynos.net in your browser settings
  4.  Disable ad blockers or strict privacy extensions temporarily
