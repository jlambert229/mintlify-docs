Each session or class uses one source: **Upload** (file from your device) or **Notes Only** (no video). Upload from [app.kaynos.net](https://app.kaynos.net) when you create or edit it.


<Info>
  Format and size limits: [Supported formats and limits](/formats).
</Info>


## Your first upload

A **session** is for one assigned student (private lesson, individual review). A **class** is for everyone in your account (group session, shared recording). Upload a file in either case — create them after you [sign in](https://app.kaynos.net).


<Tip>
  New to the flow? Read [Creating a session](/creating-sessions) and [Creating a class](/creating-classes) first.
</Tip>


1.  Create a new session or class, then pick **Upload** for the video source.
2.  Drag your file onto the box, or click to choose from Finder / Files.
3.  Watch the progress bar for speed and time remaining.
4.  When the green check appears, fill in title, date, student, and save.

### Recording tips

-   **Landscape orientation.** Your TV and your phone both want it.
-   **Camera slightly high** so the full training area is visible, not just lower legs.
-   **Even lighting** makes small details easier to see on review.


<Tip>
  Setting up a phone or camera for repeated recording? [Recording setup](/recording-setup) covers tripods, mics, lighting, and settings worth knowing.
</Tip>


## Large files

Files over **20 MB** use chunked multipart upload (**20 MB** per chunk) with parallel connections. Failed parts retry automatically (up to **3 attempts** per chunk) with exponential backoff.

## Requirements

-   Formats: MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV (convert others before uploading).
-   Max 5 GB per video.
-   Admins and coaches only.

## During upload

The UI shows percent complete, current speed, time remaining, and a **Cancel** button.

## Replace a video

Open the session or class detail page. In the video section above the notes panel, click the **×** on the current video to remove it, then upload a replacement using the same flow. The previous file is deleted from storage.


<Warning>
  **Replacing video is destructive.** The old file is deleted from storage with no undo, and **every note attached to that video is permanently deleted along with it** — notes do not migrate to the new upload and cannot be recovered. Archive the video and copy any notes you want to keep before replacing.
</Warning>


<Accordion title="Upload troubleshooting">
  ## Video upload problems

  Large uploads depend on stable internet and a supported format. Work through the checks below before assuming a bug.


  <Info>
    Reference: [Supported formats and limits](/formats).
  </Info>


  ### Fails immediately

  **"Please select a video file":** the file isn't MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV. Convert to MP4 with [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) or [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/), then try again.

  **File too big:** the limit is 5 GB. Trim the clip or export at 1080p — HandBrake's "Fast 1080p30" preset works well.

  ### Progress appears stuck

  1.  Large files can pause between chunks for 30–60 seconds — normal.
  2.  Confirm the network works (open another site).
  3.  Cancel and start again if it doesn't advance after 2 minutes.

  ### Fails partway through

  -   **Network error during part upload:** reconnect and retry.
  -   **Part upload failed: 403:** on long uploads the session can expire. Cancel and upload again.
  -   **500 server error:** wait briefly and retry; if it persists, email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net).

  ### Slow uploads

  Measure your **upload** speed, not download. [fast.com](https://fast.com) shows upload speed after the initial result.

  A wired connection often beats crowded Wi-Fi. Pause heavy sync tools (Dropbox, Google Drive) and upload at a quieter time.

  ### Status stuck on "uploading"

  If the browser closed mid-upload, email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with the session or class name and we'll reset the status.

  ### Still failing

  Email [support@kaynos.net](mailto:support@kaynos.net) with: account name, browser, file type and size, and a screenshot if you have one.
